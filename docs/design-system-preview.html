Orchestrate agents with cobalt precision.

A preview of the Relay design language: white canvas, cobalt (#2f54eb) as the single brand action color, Geist Mono as the identity signal, tight geometry, and the remaining color reserved for status. In the Linear / Vercel / Resend lineage.

Cobalt action, color for status.

Cobalt carries every action and lifts to periwinkle in dark mode. The remaining hues are status — success, danger, warning; the former info blue folds into the brand.
