Design document review notes.

The system direction is coherent: single accent, calm type, large section rhythm, and one memorable dark product-mockup pattern.

Strong foundation

The color, shape, and section-rhythm rules are specific enough to produce consistent marketing pages without over-specifying every layout.

Copy alignment

CTA language now stays in Relay's orchestration/control-plane domain rather than borrowing finance-oriented phrasing from an earlier reference.

Token clarity

`surface-dark-elevated` is documented as a deeper product-card surface, matching the visual relationship between `#16181c` and `#18232d`.

A restrained visual system for serious agent operations.

Keep Relay Blue rare, keep display type quiet, and make the dark product surface the signature moment users remember.
