# Relay Logo Redesign — "Forward Flow" Chevron Mark

**Date:** 2026-06-19
**Status:** Approved (design), pending implementation
**Supersedes:** the monoline "R" mark (`relay-mark.svg`, `relay-logo.svg`, `favicon.svg`, `RelayMark.tsx`) and the 2026-06-08 logo polish.

## Goal

Replace the current monoline "R" — which reads as a generic letterform and is thin/muddy at favicon size — with a distinctive, scalable mark that expresses Relay's core idea: **agents passing work forward in a relay.**

## Concept

**Forward Flow:** two chevrons (`⟩⟩`) pointing right — momentum, throughput, "passing it on." The leading chevron is solid/accent; the trailing chevron is a faded echo, giving motion and depth. Chosen over more literal directions (baton hand-off, signal pulse, orbital node) because it is the most legible at 16px and instantly recognizable.

## The system

Two related forms. The **badge** is the containerized app identity; the **bare mark** is the in-chrome identity. They share the same chevron geometry.

### 1. Badge — favicon & app icon

A solid blue rounded-square with the duo chevrons knocked out in white.

- Container: `rect x=4 y=4 width=56 height=56 rx=15` filled `#0052ff` (in a `0 0 64 64` viewBox).
- Chevrons (white, `stroke-width 6.5`, round caps/joins):
  - Lead (solid): `M22 20 L36 32 L22 44`
  - Tail (faded, `opacity 0.55`): `M36 20 L50 32 L36 44`
- Used **identically in light and dark** — the blue square carries its own contrast.

### 2. Bare mark — in-app (sidebar, login, inline)

The chevrons only, no square, so they sit lightly in the chrome and adapt to theme.

- viewBox `0 0 64 64`, chevrons centered, `stroke-width 7`, round caps/joins:
  - Lead (accent): `M16 14 L36 32 L16 50` — stroke `var(--color-primary)` (standalone SVG: `#0052ff`, bumped to `#3d7bff` under `prefers-color-scheme: dark` for contrast).
  - Tail (ink, `opacity 0.5`): `M36 14 L56 32 L36 50` — stroke `currentColor` (inherits text color → ink in light, white in dark).

### 3. Wordmark lockup — `relay-logo.svg`

Bare mark + "Relay" set in Inter, matching the existing lockup's type treatment (`font-weight 400`, ink via `--relay-ink` with the dark-mode override). Mark on the left, wordmark baseline-aligned.

## Color

Keep the existing brand blue `#0052ff` (already the app's `--color-primary` / accent). Dark-mode bump to `#3d7bff` only for the bare-mark lead chevron in standalone SVGs (where it sits on a dark background); the badge keeps `#0052ff` because its chevrons are white-on-blue. Ink stays `#18232d` → `#ffffff` in dark, via `currentColor` in-app and the `--relay-ink` media query in standalone SVGs.

## Out of scope (YAGNI)

- No color-palette overhaul beyond the existing `#0052ff`.
- No animated/Lottie variant of the chevrons.
- No new wordmark typeface — keep Inter.
- No PNG/ICO raster exports unless a consumer needs them (SVG favicon already in use).

## Acceptance

- Favicon and `relay-mark.svg` render as a crisp blue badge with white duo chevrons, legible at 16px.
- In-app `RelayMark` shows the bare chevrons, correct in both light and dark themes, next to the "Relay" wordmark in the sidebar, login, and inline call sites — no clipping, balanced optical size.
- `relay-logo.svg` lockup reads cleanly in light and dark.
- No raw geometry regressions: all four assets share the same chevron silhouette.
